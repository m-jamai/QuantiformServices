Data Protection & Privacy Policy

Quantiform is committed to maintaining strict data privacy compliance across all remote mechatronics and computational pipelines under Moroccan Law n° 09-08 (CNDP) and European GDPR frameworks.

1. Regulatory Framework Core

Your data submissions are managed dynamically under a combined dual-compliance environment:

  • Moroccan Framework: Dahir n° 1-09-15 enacting Law n° 09-08 relating to the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data, as regulated by the National Commission for the Protection of Personal Data (CNDP).
  • European Union Framework: Regulation (EU) 2016/679 General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) governing remote engineering services exported directly to EU operators.

2. Scope of Collected Engineering Telemetry

Data Category Specific Field Entries Legal Basis for Collection
Identity Parameters Full client name, corporate entity, active electronic mail channels. Execution of pre-contractual diagnostic loops.
Technical Telemetry Engineering bottlenecks summaries, system architecture logs, constraint values. Legitimate business analysis to map feasibility sprints.

3. Data Retention Boundaries

Operational diagnostic request files and data parameters uploaded to our initialization networks are archived safely for a period of up to [36 Months] following the final interaction pass, unless converted into an active operational project contract repository.

4. Your Regulatory Statutory Rights

Under active protocols, you have the full statutory right to invoke your rights of: access, rectification, erasure, and objection. To request any modification, transmit an explicit cryptographic identity verification request directly to compliance@quantiform.ma.
